About

Engineer across layers

I started with microcontrollers and a race car, and kept moving up the stack: from firmware to APIs to machine learning on satellite imagery.

I'm a third-year Computer Engineering student in Pune. My work spans three areas that rarely sit together: embedded and real-time systems, full-stack product engineering, and applied machine learning. I like projects where I have to understand the whole path, from raw signal to decision.

Right now I'm an ML & Data Science intern at Klimaforge, a Norway-based remote team, working on forest biomass estimation from satellites. Alongside that I'm building AarthFit, a SaaS product for small businesses, and researching lightweight intrusion detection for IoT.

I also use AI coding assistants (Claude Code, Codex) as part of my daily workflow, and I treat their output the way I treat any code: tested and reviewed.
