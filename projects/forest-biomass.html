Forest Biomass Mapping — Estonia

Satellite-fusion model that produced Estonia's national 2023 above-ground biomass map, validated with spatial-block cross-validation.

The problem

Estimate above-ground forest biomass (t/ha) across Estonia from freely available satellite data, without relying on LiDAR.

What I built

  • A model on ~678k samples and 48 engineered features fused from Sentinel-1 SAR, Sentinel-2 multispectral and terrain data (elevation, slope, aspect), with seasonal composites.
  • XGBoost reached R² 0.61 (RMSE 28.7 t/ha) on a held-out split and 0.58 cross-validated; the deployed model produced the national 2023 biomass map.
  • A later 31-feature fusion model reached cross-validated R² 0.64 on 658k samples.
  • Automated satellite data acquisition and preprocessing with Google Earth Engine, the Copernicus Data Space Ecosystem and openEO.

Honest validation

Random splits overstate accuracy when neighbouring pixels are correlated. I re-evaluated under 10 km spatial-block cross-validation (R² 0.50), and that became the reported benchmark.

Negative results that mattered

  • Benchmarked the Prithvi-EO-2.0 geospatial foundation model, fine-tuned and as frozen embeddings. It underperformed engineered features at every training size, so the classical model shipped.
  • Caught and discarded a canopy-height feature that leaked the target (R² 1.00).
  • Tested a log-target variant (R² 0.53) and did not deploy it.
