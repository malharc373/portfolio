Collections Recovery — Claim Verification

Tested the claim that recovery improved 11% month-on-month. It did not: recovery is flat.

  • Python
  • SQL
  • Jupyter
  • Pandas
View on GitHub ↗
Reported claim
+11% MoM
Measured
+0.18% MoM
Window
Jan – Aug 2026
Verdict
Not supported

Finding

Deduplicated successful recovery by month stayed between roughly ₹17 and ₹19 Cr. The 11% figure only reproduces under a denominator that permanently removes accounts once they reach a closed status.

That rule is contradicted by the data: 44.7% of closed accounts later reopen, and ₹44.3 Cr was collected after first closure. Under that rule the account base collapses by half while collections stay flat, so the rate rises arithmetically.

Scope corrections

  • The data covers 1 Jan – 8 Aug 2026, not the 12 months in the brief, so MoM means are over six transitions.
  • August is about a quarter of a month and was excluded from MoM comparisons.

Deliverables

SQL repository, analysis notebook, golden-dataset pipeline with a row ledger, data-quality report and an executive dashboard.
